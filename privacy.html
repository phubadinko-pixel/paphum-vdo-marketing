Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

Paphum Petroleum (Bangchak, Lamphun Industrial Estate Junction, Thailand) operates the internal tool "vdo-marketing". Contact: 053-581512.

What the app does

vdo-marketing lets our staff upload our own promotional videos to our own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we handle

  • When our authorized staff member signs in with TikTok, we receive an access token, refresh token and the account's open ID. These are stored only in a file on the staff member's own computer and are used only to call the TikTok API for that account.
  • The videos and captions we upload are our own marketing content, taken from files on our own computer.

We do not collect, store or process personal data of TikTok viewers or other TikTok users. We have no website accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers on this site.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, to perform the actions our staff request.

Retention and your control

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local token file. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services (Manage app permissions).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
