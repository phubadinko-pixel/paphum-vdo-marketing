Paphum Petroleum – vdo-marketing

Paphum Petroleum (Bangchak station, Lamphun Industrial Estate Junction) is a gas station offering fuel, free showers, coin-operated laundry and car wash, a convenience store, food and a coffee shop, open 24 hours.

vdo-marketing is a small internal desktop tool used only by the station's own staff. It uploads the station's own short promotional videos to the station's own TikTok account, using TikTok's Content Posting API. It has no public sign-up and no other users.
